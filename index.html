FAQ

What is Game5?

A gaming network where players play fast, verifiable games and earn rewards on Robinhood Chain, powered by the $GAME5 token.

Which wallets are supported?

Any EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX). Game5 prompts your wallet to add Robinhood Chain (chain ID 4663) automatically.

Are games fair?

Each round derives its result from a server seed, client seed and nonce. The seed hash is shown before you play so the result can be verified afterwards.

Where is the contract address?

The official CA is listed in the $GAME5 section and announced only on @Game5Fun. Ignore any other source.

Is there an age or region restriction?

You must be of legal age and allowed to play games of chance where you live. Play responsibly.

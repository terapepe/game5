FAQ

What is Game5?

A gaming network where players play fast, verifiable games and earn rewards on five blockchains, powered by the $GAME5 token.

Which wallets are supported?

Any injected EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, OKX) for Ethereum, Robinhood Chain, Base and BSC; Phantom or Solflare for Solana.

Are games fair?

Each round derives its result from a server seed, client seed and nonce. The seed hash is shown before you play so the result can be verified afterwards.

Where is the contract address?

Official CAs are listed in the $GAME5 section and announced only on @Game5Network. Ignore any other source.

Is there an age or region restriction?

You must be of legal age and allowed to play games of chance where you live. Play responsibly.
